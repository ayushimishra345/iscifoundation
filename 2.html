Who We Are

Empowering Communities, Sustaining the Planet

The zeal to help underprivileged students in getting education laid the foundation of ISCI Foundation. We believe that no one has made it through life without someone else's help — and we're here to be that help.

We are a GuideStar India certified non-profit organisation working towards rural transformation, health, education, sport for development, culture & heritage, and women empowerment across India.
